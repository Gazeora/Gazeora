## When you need a commercial license

The GPL already lets anyone use, sell and distribute Gazeora, including for money, as long as
they keep to its terms. You need a commercial license if you want to do something the GPL
version 3 and [NOTICE](NOTICE) don't allow, for example:

- ship Gazeora, or a product built on it or containing part of its code, without giving its
  users the complete source under the GPL version 3;
- build Gazeora or its code into proprietary software, hardware or a VR headset, viewer or
  bundle you sell;
- keep your changes to Gazeora private while distributing them;
- leave out the attribution "Gazeora by Nithin Aashik", or not mark a modified version, as
  [NOTICE](NOTICE) requires;
- have terms the GPL can't give, such as a warranty, support or indemnity.

If you're unsure whether your use needs one, ask: it costs nothing to check.
