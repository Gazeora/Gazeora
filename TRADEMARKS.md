# Name and logo

Gazeora's code is free software under the GNU GPL version 3 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE) and
[NOTICE](NOTICE)). The name **Gazeora**, its logo and its icon are not covered by that license:
they identify the original project by Nithin Aashik.

## Fine without asking

- Using Gazeora, unmodified or modified, for yourself.
- Sharing unmodified copies of Gazeora under its own name.
- Saying truthfully that your version is "based on Gazeora" or "a fork of Gazeora".
- Writing about Gazeora, linking to it, reviewing it or teaching with it.

## Not fine without permission

- Calling a modified version "Gazeora", or a name easily confused with it (such as
  "Gazeora Pro" or "Gazeora Plus").
- Using the Gazeora logo or icon as the logo or icon of a modified version.
- Suggesting that your version, product or service is made or endorsed by the Gazeora
  project or Nithin Aashik.

If you share a modified version, give it a different name and icon. The attribution
"Gazeora by Nithin Aashik" must still appear as [NOTICE](NOTICE) describes.

## Asking for permission

To use the name or logo in a way this page doesn't allow, write to Nithin Aashik at
<nithinaashikps@gmail.com>, with "Gazeora name and logo" in the subject. Permission is given in
writing, and can be part of a [commercial license](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md): a product built on
Gazeora under one may carry its name or logo only if the agreement says so.
